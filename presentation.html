Executive Summary

QuantumX: Hybrid Quantum Machine Learning for Early Alzheimer's Detection

Catching neurodegeneration in the 10–15 year pre-symptomatic window with explainable quantum intelligence

Problem Statement

The Diagnostic Dilemma in Early Alzheimer's

Why traditional clinical testing and classical machine learning struggle

⏳ 10–15 Year Latency

Brain damage begins more than a decade before noticeable memory loss.

  • Subtle brain atrophy (nWBV) and slight cognitive drops (MMSE) happen quietly.
  • No single clinical measurement tells the full story on its own.

📉 Small Clinical Cohorts

Medical data is inherently limited and expensive to acquire:

  • Classical deep neural networks easily overfit on small patient samples.
  • Linear models fail to capture non-linear interactions across age and brain volume.

🚫 The "Black Box" Barrier

Doctors cannot act on opaque AI predictions:

  • Prescribing aggressive preventive medication requires biological evidence.
  • Physicians need to know why a model flagged a patient as high-risk.
The Solution

What We Built: The QuantumX Platform

A fully implemented, validated, and reproducible software pipeline

⚛️ Dual Quantum Classification Engines

  • Hybrid Variational Quantum Classifier (VQC): 4 qubits, 3 layers, and 36 variational parameters trained using exact hardware-compatible gradients (Parameter-Shift Rule).
  • Quantum Support Vector Machine (QSVM): Non-linear IQP ZZ feature map computing quantum state fidelity overlap between patient pairs.
  • Dual Framework Support: Fully implemented in PennyLane for optimization and Qiskit 2.6 for IBM Quantum hardware readiness.

🔍 The QuantumXAI Explainability Suite

  • Quantum Feature Attribution (QFA): Measures individual biomarker impact per patient (e.g., proving whether MMSE or brain volume drove the risk).
  • Von Neumann Entanglement Entropy: Mathematically verifies that quantum entanglement actively connected the patient's multi-modal biomarkers.
  • Interactive Streamlit UI: A clean dashboard allowing doctors to test patient samples or simulate custom biomarker changes in real time.
Architecture Choices

Why We Chose Each Component

Clear technical rationale behind our quantum design decisions

💡 Encoding & Qubit Count

  • Why 4 Qubits? Maps 4 core clinical biomarkers (MMSE, nWBV, eTIV, Age) into a 2⁴ = 16-dimensional quantum state. Keeps circuit depth shallow to minimize noise on NISQ hardware.
  • Why R_Y Angle Encoding? Requires depth O(1) with zero multi-qubit entangling gates for state loading, avoiding the exponential CNOT gate overhead of Amplitude Encoding.

⚡ Circuit Design & Training

  • Why Strongly Entangling Layers? Universal 3-axis single-qubit rotations (R_Z R_Y R_Z) paired with circular CNOT ladders provide high expressivity without barren plateau issues.
  • Why the Parameter-Shift Rule? Finite-difference gradients fail on real quantum hardware due to shot noise. The Parameter-Shift rule evaluates exact mathematical gradients directly on quantum chips.

Quantum Core

The Quantum Engine: Plain & Mathematical

How quantum mechanics processes multi-biomarker relationships

⚛️ Hybrid VQC (Variational Classifier)

What it does: Rotates and entangles 4 qubits using 36 adjustable parameters, learning the boundary between health and dementia.

Circuit: 3 Layers × 4 Qubits × 3 Rotations = 36 Parameters Readout: ŷ = ⟨ψ(x)| Z₀ |ψ(x)⟩ ∈ [-1, 1]

Exact Gradient (Parameter-Shift Rule):

∂⟨Z₀⟩/∂θ = [ ⟨Z₀⟩(θ + π/2) - ⟨Z₀⟩(θ - π/2) ] / 2

🌐 QSVM (Quantum Support Vector Machine)

What it does: Maps patient data into high-dimensional Hilbert space using an IQP ZZ feature map and measures quantum state overlap.

Kernel Matrix: K(x_i, x_j) = |⟨Φ(x_i)|Φ(x_j)⟩|² Captures non-linear cross-feature interactions

Qiskit 2.6 Native Circuit:

Built with ParameterVector and efficient_su2 Simulated on Qiskit Statevector (⟨Z₀⟩ = 0.2252)
Explainable AI

QuantumXAI: Why Doctors Can Trust It

Transforming quantum operations into transparent clinical insights

🔍 1. Feature Attribution

Which biomarker drove the diagnosis?

Uses in-circuit angle perturbation (Δ⟨Z₀⟩ / Δx_i) to quantify how much each factor contributed to the risk score.

Example: Shows that cognitive drop (MMSE) contributed 55% while brain atrophy (nWBV) contributed 35%.

🌀 2. Entanglement Entropy

Were features evaluated together?

Computes Von Neumann Entropy S(ρ_A) via Schmidt decomposition of the quantum state.

Physical proof that the circuit evaluated entangled multi-modal patterns rather than isolated variables.

🛡️ 3. Barren Plateau Safety

Is the quantum model reliable?

Tracks gradient variance across random initializations.

Confirms non-zero gradient variance, proving the model operates safely outside vanishing gradient zones.

Future Outlook

Honest Limitations & Next Steps

Our path from working prototype to clinical practice

⚠️ Honest Limitations

  • Cohort Scale: Validated on Disc 1 of OASIS-1 (39 patients); scaling to all 416 subjects requires batch quantum kernel processing.
  • Simulator vs Hardware: Tested on Statevector simulators. Real quantum chips introduce hardware decoherence and readout errors.

🚀 3-Phase Roadmap

  • Phase 1 (Immediate): Deploy on IBM Quantum hardware with Zero-Noise Extrapolation (ZNE) error mitigation via Mitiq.
  • Phase 2 (6 Months): Implement Quantum Convolutional Neural Networks (QCNN) directly on raw 3D MRI brain slices.
  • Phase 3 (12 Months): Multi-hospital clinical validation and federated quantum learning.
