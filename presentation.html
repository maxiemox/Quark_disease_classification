  • Uninterpretable weights provide zero medical rationale for why a patient is at risk.

Slide 4 · Design Rationale (The "Why")

Why We Chose Each Component

Rigorous technical justification for our quantum architecture and design trade-offs

💡 Encoding & Qubit Choices

  • Why 4 Qubits? Maps 4 core clinical features (MMSE, nWBV, eTIV, Age) into a 2⁴ = 16-dimensional Hilbert space. Keeps circuit shallow (depth 3) to minimize decoherence on NISQ hardware.
  • Why R_Y Angle Encoding over Amplitude Encoding? Angle encoding requires O(1) circuit depth and zero multi-qubit entangling gates for state preparation, preserving qubit coherence.

⚡ Circuit & Optimization Choices

  • Why Strongly Entangling Layers? Universal 3-parameter single-qubit rotations (R_Z R_Y R_Z) + circular CNOT ladders provide maximal expressivity to capture cross-biomarker correlations.
  • Why Parameter-Shift over Finite Differences? Finite differences fail under quantum shot noise. The Parameter-Shift rule computes exact analytic gradients on real quantum chips.
Judges Script: "Every architectural choice was deliberate: 4 qubits avoids barren plateaus, angle encoding keeps depth minimal for NISQ hardware, and the parameter-shift rule guarantees exact gradients without finite-difference noise."

The Quantum Engine: Circuits & Formulations

Exact mathematical implementations verified in code

⚛️ Hybrid VQC Formulation

|ψ(x)⟩ = ( ∏ CNOT_ladder ∏ U(α,β,γ) ) ⊗ R_Y(x_i)|0⟩ Parameters = 3 layers × 4 qubits × 3 angles = 36 params Readout: ŷ = ⟨ψ(x,θ)| Z₀ |ψ(x,θ)⟩ ∈ [-1, 1]

Parameter-Shift Rule (Exact Gradients on Hardware):

∂⟨Z₀⟩/∂θ_k = ( ⟨Z₀⟩_{θ_k + π/2} - ⟨Z₀⟩_{θ_k - π/2} ) / 2

Slide 7 · Key Innovation

QuantumXAI: Opening the Quantum Black Box

Empowering clinicians with physical and biological explainability

🎯 1. Feature Attribution

In-Circuit Angle Perturbation:

Perturbs each biomarker angle: Δ⟨Z₀⟩ / Δx_i to quantify its diagnostic weight.

Proves to clinicians whether cognitive score (MMSE) or brain atrophy (nWBV) drove the decision.

🌀 2. Entanglement Entropy

Von Neumann Entropy S(ρ_A):

S(ρ_A) = -∑ λ_k² log₂(λ_k²) via Schmidt SVD decomposition

Mathematically verifies that qubits became entangled, proving cross-biomarker quantum correlations were used.

🛡️ 3. Barren Plateau Monitor

Trainability Diagnostic:

Calculates gradient variance Var[∇_θ L] across random initializations.

Verifies non-zero variance, proving the model operates far from vanishing gradient regimes.

Slide 10 · Roadmap & Conclusion

Limitations, Future Roadmap & Conclusion

The roadmap from NISQ prototype to clinical production

⚠️ Honest Limitations

  • Cohort Scale: Evaluated on Disc 1 of OASIS-1 (39 patients); scaling to full 416 subjects requires distributed kernel batching.
  • Simulator Execution: Evaluated on Statevector simulators. Physical hardware introduces thermal relaxation (T₁) and dephasing (T₂).

🚀 3-Phase Production Roadmap

  • Phase 1 (Immediate): Deploy with Zero-Noise Extrapolation (ZNE) error mitigation via Mitiq on IBM Quantum QPUs.
  • Phase 2 (6 Months): Implement Quantum Convolutional Neural Networks (QCNN) directly on 3D MPRAGE raw brain voxel slices.
  • Phase 3 (12 Months): Multi-center clinical validation and federated quantum learning across hospital networks.
